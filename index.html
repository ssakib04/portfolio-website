Systems, Not

A quick read on how I got here and what I actually spend my time doing.

I'm a versatile AI and web developer with a track record of building intelligent systems and streamlining workflows. My work spans front-end development, process automation, and machine learning, with particular depth in AI agents and no-code orchestration tools like Make.com and n8n.

I'm currently an AI Trainee at BJIT, building hands-on experience in applied AI development inside a professional engineering environment. Outside of client and employer work, I run my own experiments: chatbot-driven task agents, RAG systems over PDFs, and interfaces for tools that used to require a spreadsheet and a lot of patience. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BRAC University, graduating on the VC's List and Dean's List.
